Before you use IFR Go/No-Go

This is a free decision aid for instrument pilots flying under FAA Part 91. It is not an official weather briefing, flight plan or approved source of aeronautical information.

  • As pilot in command you must get all available information for the flight (§91.103), including an official briefing, NOTAMs and TFRs.
  • Weather, winds, cloud, icing and instability come from public forecasts and models that can be wrong, late or missing.
  • Time, fuel and performance are estimates. Check them against your POH.
  • The go/no-go result is only as good as the numbers you enter. The decision is always yours.
